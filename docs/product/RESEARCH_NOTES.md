# Product Feature Research Incorporated

The package incorporates these proven product patterns from current document-knowledge and professional-review systems:

## Source-grounded interaction

- Source selection controls so users can include or exclude sources for a question.
- Direct citations built from source quotes and exact locations.
- Citation interactions that reveal the supporting text and navigate to its context.
- Source-only answering with explicit abstention when the selected knowledge cannot support an answer.

Reference: Google Gemini Notebook help, `https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16179559`

## High-volume review

- Large document-set organization and analysis.
- Structured review/evidence tables for comparing documents and extracting recurring facts.
- Conditional follow-up review logic.
- Upload failure logs and recoverable retry paths.
- Collaboration and controlled sharing.

References:

- Harvey Vault, `https://www.harvey.ai/platform/vault`
- Harvey Vault help, `https://help.harvey.ai/topics/vault`
- Harvey product update on upload failure logs, `https://www.harvey.ai/blog/the-brief-may-2025`

## Permission-aware enterprise knowledge

- Retrieval constrained by the user's access rights.
- Cited answers grounded in approved company sources.
- Knowledge connectors and source traceability.
- Knowledge graph/relationship context as an optional future enhancement.

References:

- Glean enterprise knowledge overview, `https://www.glean.com/product/all-knowledge`
- Glean knowledge assistant overview, `https://www.glean.com/perspectives/top-ai-assistants-for-leveraging-company-knowledge`

## UXE-specific improvements beyond the benchmark

- Three explicit evidence-depth modes: Yes / No, Optimal, Details + references.
- Deterministic citation verification and evidence coverage.
- Requirement-by-requirement compliance analysis instead of a single free-form answer.
- Exact page/clause/paragraph/bounding-box locators.
- Non-destructive, review-first corrected-document workflow.
- Same-format corrected output with signature and OCR limitations handled honestly.
- Source-version lineage so historical consultation citations remain reproducible.
- Product-visible processing stages, model/connector health, and retry controls.
